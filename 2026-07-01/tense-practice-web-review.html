Decision routine

  • Identify whether the sentence describes an action in progress, a repeated fact, a completed past event, or an earlier past event.
  • Use the time marker and event order before choosing the tense; do not choose only by sound.
  • Check whether a temporary action needs a continuous form and whether one earlier action needs the past perfect.

Forms to keep automatic

  • Present continuous: am, is, or are + verb-ing for an action in progress or a changing situation.
  • Present simple: base form or third-person -s for repeated actions, facts, and stable states.
  • Past continuous: was or were + verb-ing for an action already in progress at a past time.
  • Present perfect: have or has + past participle when a past action remains connected to the present.

Correction habit

  • For every corrected item, say the time clue, name the event relationship, and then give the form.
  • A blank response is not evidence of mastery; return to the sentence and complete the full reasoning chain.
