Joy Lesson 09: 45Q Mixed Practice

Transitions II + Rhetorical Synthesis + Sentence Combining

Directions

For transition questions, choose the word or phrase that most precisely expresses the writer's move. For synthesis and combining questions, choose the sentence that best matches the stated goal.
