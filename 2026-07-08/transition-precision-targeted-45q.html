Directions

Choose the transition that most precisely completes the text. For the first 10 questions, use the hints to mark anchor words, shorten the ideas, and identify the sentence skeleton before choosing.

Part 1: Scaffolded Questions With Hints

Question 1
Hinted

The Hall Street archive was created to preserve the neighborhood's history of jazz performance. ______ its reading room is decorated with enlarged photographs of musicians who once played in nearby clubs.

Anchor words: created to preserve, decorated with photographs of musicians

Shortened ideas: archive purpose = preserve jazz history; room design = shows local musicians.

Skeleton: archive was created to preserve history; reading room is decorated with musician photos.

A. Fittingly,
B. For example,
C. Consequently,
D. Nevertheless,

A. Fittingly,

C. For example,
D. By comparison,
