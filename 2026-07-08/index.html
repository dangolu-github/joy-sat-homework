Class review

The English review preserved from the class record.

Focus: advanced transition precision after Homework review and selected Test 4 mock-exam mistakes.

Class Content

Reviewed homework mistakes and explained two basic mistakes from the previous Test 4 mock exam.

Moved from basic transition categories into more difficult transition precision, since Joy handled the basic level well.

Practiced choosing the exact transition inside one relationship family, especially subtle choices such as “in fact,” “specifically,” and “fittingly.”

Worked on contrast, concession, replacement, and comparison, with special attention to not relying only on keywords.

Practiced shortening long sentences into core skeletons: subject, verb, object, and the two ideas around the blank.

Student Performance

Joy stayed engaged, cooperative, and willing to try even when the distinction was uncertain.

She showed a good basic sense of when there was no cause/effect or no direct contrast.

She showed useful self-awareness when she noticed that some mistakes came from reading too quickly or not processing the whole context.

She could sometimes identify that contrast is not the same as replacement, especially when the sentence did not allow “not X, but Y.”

Main difficulty: precise transition choice in close categories, especially “fittingly” vs example/cause-effect and concession vs replacement.

Joy still needs more independence in explaining why an answer works, rather than waiting for the tutor to confirm the logic.

Improvement Plan

Before choosing an answer, Joy should reduce the sentence to two short ideas and state the relationship in her own words.

For “fittingly,” ask: “Does the second idea appropriately match the purpose, role, name, or design of the first idea?”

For example transitions, check whether the second idea is simply one case of a broader claim.

For cause/effect transitions, check whether the first idea directly produces the second idea.

For concession, use the pattern: limitation or expectation, but the main idea still holds.

For replacement, use the pattern: not X; rather/instead Y.

Complete the new 45-question transition precision targeted practice.

Prepare to review earlier mock-exam mistakes and be ready for the next comprehensive mock when assigned.
