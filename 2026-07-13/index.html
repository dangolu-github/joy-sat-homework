July 13, 2026 · Reading Mini-Unit: Central Ideas and Details

Class Content

Reviewed selected Class 10 transition work, then shifted to Central Ideas and Details: task recognition, main point versus support, and answer scope.

Used a fixed routine: identify the topic, add the “so what” point, then check whether an answer covers the whole passage rather than one detail.

Rejected answers that were too broad, too narrow, distorted, or based on new information.

Student Performance

She identified general topics and contrasting information quickly and reported that the unit felt too easy.

The next growth point is precision: explain the logic clearly and state exactly what is confusing. Independent work will confirm whether accuracy matches her confidence.

Improvement Plan

Raise the starting difficulty; repeat basic Central Ideas instruction only if returned work shows a specific error pattern.

For main idea, predict: “This is about ___, and the main point is ___.” Reject answers limited to one example or detail.

For evidence, underline the claim, predict what would prove or weaken it, and choose the most direct link. For uncertain items, explain why the answer works and the closest trap fails.

Homework / Next Steps

Complete the 20Q Central Ideas practice and 12Q official Command of Evidence preview. Mark missed or uncertain items; those will guide Class 12.

Complete mock test 2 (66Q) in three sittings of 22. Check after all three; send the score, misses, and uncertain items.
