Lesson 2 SAT-Style Demonstrations

Form, Structure, and Sense in long sentences

Color Key

subject / antecedent finite verb time clue nonfinite verb modifier target

1. Main Verb + Purpose Infinitive

In 1990, after several years of work on optical systems and robotic sensors at a NASA research center, engineer Ellen Ochoa left her position to join the space agency's astronaut training program.

finite main verb: left | purpose infinitive: to join

2. Finite Main Verb Needed

Although botanists recognize more than fifty species of sunflower, the silverleaf sunflower, a species found in parts of the southeastern United States, includes both an early-flowering coastal ecotype and a late-flowering inland ecotype.

subject: the silverleaf sunflower | finite main verb: includes

3. Simple Present For General Facts

Unlike a single economic statistic, India's Ease of Living Index measures several dimensions of public well-being, including economic opportunity, environmental sustainability, and residents' access to basic services.

general function: index + measures

4. Simple Past For Completed Historical Action

In the late 1960s, African American abstract painter Sam Gilliam applied bold, saturated colors to unstretched canvases and suspended the painted fabric from ceilings and walls.

same subject and same time frame: applied + suspended

5. Past Perfect For Earlier Past Action

In 1881, French chemist Camille Faure redesigned the rechargeable lead-acid battery that physicist Gaston Plante had invented fifteen years earlier, greatly increasing the amount of electricity the battery could store.

later past action: redesigned | earlier past action: had invented

6. Hidden Singular Subject

Janaki Ammal's chromosomal survey of plants in Silent Valley, a tropical forest in Kerala that contains nearly one thousand species of native flora, was instrumental in the government's decision to preserve the forest.

true subject: survey | verb: was

7. Hidden Plural Subject

The artistic talents of Barbara Chase-Riboud, an author widely known for a historical novel that inspired public discussion, are not limited to prose; she first became recognized for sculpture.

true subject: talents | verb: are

8. Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement

Because Layli Long Soldier's star quilt poems arrange eight panels of text in a traditional Lakota pattern, the poems invite readers to choose their own route through the work rather than follow a single fixed order.

antecedent: poems | possessive determiner: their

9. Its vs It's

Although early scientists struggled to classify the duck-billed platypus, the animal, which lays eggs but also nurses its young with milk, is now classified as a monotreme.

antecedent: the animal | possessive pronoun: its

10. Opening Modifier Target

Far from being modern inventions, drinking straws were used by Sumerians in ancient Mesopotamia more than five thousand years ago.

modifier: Far from being modern inventions | target: drinking straws

11. Appositive Modifier Target

A coming-of-age story, a social meditation, and a sober examination of colonial power, Driss Chraibi's debut novel remains one of the most discussed works in modern North African literature.

modifier: A coming-of-age story... | target: debut novel

12. -ing Modifier After A Complete Clause

Nuhad al-Haddad, known professionally as Fairuz, built a broad repertoire that blended traditional Arabic forms with modern popular styles, earning her the moniker "the soul of Lebanon."

complete clause: built a broad repertoire | result modifier: earning
