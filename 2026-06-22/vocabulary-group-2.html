Vocabulary Group 2: Time, Change, and Evidence

Lesson 2 vocabulary routine for tense clues, historical sequence, and evidence language

How To Study This Group

  • Learn by meaning group, not alphabetically.
  • Connect time words to grammar: prior, precede, subsequent, former, latter.
  • Connect evidence words to SAT explanations: indicate, demonstrate, reveal, attribute.

Knowledge Map

Time Order

  • precede - come before something in time or order
  • subsequent - coming after something else
  • prior - earlier; before
  • former - the first of two; previous
  • latter - the second of two
  • concurrent - happening at the same time
  • successive - following one after another
  • cumulative - increasing by adding parts over time

Duration and Change

  • persistent - continuing for a long time
  • transient - lasting only a short time
  • enduring - lasting for a long time
  • emerge - come into view or become known
  • evolve - develop gradually
  • alter - change
  • modify - change partly, not completely
  • transform - change greatly
  • preserve - keep safe or unchanged
  • sustain - keep going; support over time

Evidence and Relationship

  • indicate - show or point to
  • demonstrate - show clearly with evidence
  • reveal - make known
  • attribute - say that something was caused by or belongs to someone/something
  • correspond - match or be connected
  • contradict - say or show the opposite

Practice B: Fill in the Blank

  1. The invention of the original battery ______ Faure’s redesign by fifteen years.
  2. The researcher noticed a ______ pattern that continued across all three trials.
  3. A small change in temperature can ______ the speed of a chemical reaction.
  4. The new data ______ that the earlier theory was incomplete.
  5. The artist produced three ______ versions of the same design, each more abstract than the last.
  6. The program was created to ______ local access to public health services.
  7. The first experiment failed, but a ______ trial produced clearer results.
  8. The evidence ______ the claim that the object was a modern invention.

B. persistent
