Class review

The English review preserved from the class record.

Form, Structure, and Sense | Grammar follow-up | Targeted punctuation homework

Class Content

Focus

Class notes

Homework review

Reviewed the previous Boundaries homework. Main weak areas: comma use with nonessential information, dash use, sentence boundaries, and semicolon/list punctuation.

Grammar focus

Introduced Form, Structure, and Sense through finite vs. nonfinite verbs, infinitives for purpose, -ing phrases for result/method, passive voice, and how to reduce a long sentence to its subject-verb skeleton.

Application

Used short practice and sentence-skeleton thinking to decide whether a choice supplies the needed verb form, punctuation mark, or sentence connection.

Student Performance

Joy was well engaged and able to ask useful clarification questions, especially about dash rules and the difference between comma and dash choices.

She needs more automatic control of comma rules: introductory phrases, nonessential information, examples with such as, and comma + FANBOYS.

She was confused by nonfinite verb structures, especially to do for purpose versus doing for result/method.

She also needs to recognize passive voice and core subject-verb structure more quickly in long SAT-style sentences.

Improvement Plan

Keep using the sentence-skeleton method: remove extra information, find the real subject and verb, then decide the punctuation or verb-form need.

Do targeted comma/dash practice before moving into harder mixed grammar so the punctuation rules become automatic.

Continue reviewing finite/nonfinite verbs and passive voice through examples.

Homework

Complete the Lesson 2 targeted comma/dash mixed punctuation homework, 30 questions.

Review the tense grammar handout and the finite/nonfinite verb notes.
