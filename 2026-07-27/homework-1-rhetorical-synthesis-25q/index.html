Rhetorical Synthesis · 25-Question Practice

Joy · Class 17 Homework 1

Three-pass routine
1 · Goal-Verb LockRecognition cue: “The student wants to…” Circle the action, audience, comparison axis, and scope.
2 · Note FilterKeep required notes; hold optional notes; reject true-but-off-goal notes.
3 · Sentence Fit TestCheck accuracy, relationship, emphasis, scope, economy, and complete goal fulfillment.

Trap rule: true is not enough. A partial goal, wrong emphasis, added claim, or all-notes dump is wrong.

Unaided transfer: before opening the choices, state the goal in one sentence and name the two or three notes the answer must use.
