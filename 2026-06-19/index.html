Class review

The English review preserved from the class record.

Class Content Summary

Class 1 mainly focused on building Joy's foundation for SAT Reading and Writing grammar. We reviewed the trial homework on tense forms and then moved into sentence boundaries, which are one of the highest-value grammar topics for SAT improvement.

The grammar work centered on identifying independent clauses, dependent clauses, fragments, run-ons, comma splices, semicolon use, comma + FANBOYS, colon use, nonessential information, and cases where no punctuation should be inserted inside one grammar unit.

The core method practiced in class was: shorten the sentence, find the subject and conjugated verb, label the structure before and after the blank, then choose punctuation based on the structure rather than sound or instinct.

Student Performance

Strengths

  • Joy was willing to participate and attempted the grammar questions even when unsure.
  • Her basic grammar intuition is solid; the trial homework result showed a good starting point for foundational grammar.
  • She could recognize the idea of an independent clause as a complete thought.
  • She showed good recall of simple sentence-separation logic, especially when a period could separate two complete sentences.
  • She was receptive to correction and adjusted her thinking when guided step by step.

Improvement Plan For Later Classes

  1. Joy can often choose a reasonable answer, but she needs to explain why the answer is correct using the rule.
  2. Advanced tense reasoning needs more practice, especially past continuous, the sequence of past actions, and perfect tense forms.
  3. Joy needs to judge semicolon, colon, comma, and period by sentence structure rather than by which punctuation mark feels more formal.

Homework

  1. Complete the 30-question Boundaries Practice. For each missed or uncertain question, mark the structure before choosing the answer.
  2. Optional: Study Vocabulary Group 1: Helpful and Harmful. Complete the matching, fill-in-the-blank, and multiple-choice practice.
