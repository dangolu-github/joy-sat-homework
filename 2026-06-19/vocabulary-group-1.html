Vocabulary Group 1: Helpful and Harmful

Digital SAT vocabulary handout for Joy

Adapted from the first thematic group in Robert's Extremely Nerdy Guide to Digital SAT Vocabulary. Definitions, examples, and practice are rewritten for class use.

How To Study This Group

  • Learn the words by meaning group first, not alphabetically.
  • For each word, ask: is it helpful, harmful, blocking, supporting, weakening, or practical?
  • For SAT Words-in-Context, the sentence usually gives the emotional direction: positive, negative, neutral, or practical.

Knowledge Map

Helpful / Improving

  • beneficial - helpful; producing a good result
  • advantageous - useful because it gives an advantage
  • constructive - helpful in a way that improves something
  • conducive - making a result easier or more likely

SAT cue: positive effect, improvement, good result.

Realistic / Practical

  • practical - realistic; useful in real situations
  • pragmatic - focused on what works, not theory or ideals

SAT cue: workable, realistic, not abstract.

Strengthen / Support

  • reinforce - strengthen or support
  • bolster - support or make stronger
  • buttress - support with evidence or structure
  • undergird - provide the basic support or foundation

SAT cue: evidence, foundation, support, argument strength.

Use / Take Advantage Of

  • utilize - use for a practical purpose
  • employ - use a method, skill, or tool
  • exploit - use for advantage, often selfishly or unfairly
  • leverage - use something strategically to get a result

SAT cue: method, tool, resource, strategic use.

Speed Up / Assist

  • expedite - make a process happen faster
  • facilitate - make a process easier
  • accommodate - adjust to meet a need or request

SAT cue: process becomes easier, faster, or better supported.

Relevant / Appropriate

  • apt - suitable; exactly appropriate
  • relevant - connected to the topic or situation
  • pertinent - directly related and important

SAT cue: on-topic, fitting, directly connected.

Effective / Method

  • efficacious - effective; producing the intended result
  • means - a method for achieving a result
  • medium - a channel or form used to communicate or create
  • mechanism - a process or system that makes something work

SAT cue: method, process, channel, result.

Harmless / Benefit

  • benign - gentle or not harmful
  • innocuous - harmless; not likely to offend
  • boon - something very helpful or beneficial

SAT cue: harmless, safe, useful, advantage.

Annoyance / Obstacle

  • bane - a source of trouble or misery
  • nuisance - something annoying or inconvenient
  • forestall - prevent by acting early
  • preclude - make impossible; prevent from happening

SAT cue: annoyance, prevention, blocked possibility.

Block / Slow Down

  • impede - slow progress
  • inhibit - hold back or restrain
  • hamper - make progress difficult
  • hinder - obstruct or slow down
  • thwart - successfully stop a plan
  • encumber - weigh down or burden

SAT cue: delay, block, restrain, burden.

Weaken / Put At Risk

  • undermine - gradually weaken or damage
  • compromise - weaken, damage, or put at risk
  • endanger - put in danger
  • jeopardize - put at risk

SAT cue: risk, damage, weakening, loss of safety.

Rough / Basic / Temporary

  • crude - rough, unfinished, or not refined
  • makeshift - temporary, used as a substitute
  • rudimentary - basic; not advanced

SAT cue: early version, temporary fix, basic form.

Worsen / Harmful / Dangerous

  • exacerbate - make worse or more severe
  • aggravate - make worse; irritate
  • adverse - unfavorable or harmful
  • detrimental - causing harm or damage
  • insidious - gradually harmful in a hidden way
  • pernicious - extremely harmful or destructive

SAT cue: negative effect, worsening, danger, hidden harm.

Practice C: SAT-Style Multiple Choice

1. The new irrigation system was a ______ for farmers in the dry region because it allowed crops to survive a long period with little rainfall.

A. bane
B. boon
C. nuisance
D. impediment

2. Because the first model of the device was only a ______ prototype, it showed the basic idea but lacked the precision needed for actual use.

A. crude
B. pertinent
C. efficacious
D. benign

3. The historian's new letters helped ______ the argument that the two writers had exchanged ideas for years.

A. hamper
B. buttress
C. preclude
D. aggravate

4. The storm did not merely inconvenience travelers; it ______ rescue efforts by washing out several roads.

A. facilitated
B. expedited
C. hindered
D. accommodated

5. The chemical was considered ______ in small amounts, but researchers still handled it carefully during the experiment.

A. innocuous
B. pernicious
C. adverse
D. detrimental

6. Lack of sleep can ______ stress, making it harder for students to concentrate during demanding exams.

A. accommodate
B. facilitate
C. exacerbate
D. forestall

7. The new evidence was not ______ to the debate, so the committee decided not to discuss it.

A. relevant
B. makeshift
C. insidious
D. pernicious

8. Because the bridge was unsafe, officials closed it to ______ accidents before they could occur.

A. forestall
B. exploit
C. endanger
D. encumber

9. The report warned that ignoring small cracks in the dam could ______ public safety.

A. reinforce
B. jeopardize
C. accommodate
D. expedite

10. The artist chose photography as her primary ______ because it allowed her to document city life quickly and directly.

A. medium
B. nuisance
C. bane
D. detriment

11. The medication was judged ______ because most patients improved within a week of taking it.

A. rudimentary
B. efficacious
C. adverse
D. insidious

12. The long application form may ______ students from applying for financial aid, even when they qualify for support.

A. inhibit
B. bolster
C. facilitate
D. expedite
