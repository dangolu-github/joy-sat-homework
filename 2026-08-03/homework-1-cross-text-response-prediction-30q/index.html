Homework job

For each pair, identify the exact target claim and the named responder. Predict the responder's polarity and text-based reason before testing the options. Choose one answer; then name the exact field broken by the closest distractor.

Independent work: no hints and no answers appear in the student version. Blue words are vocabulary support, never answer signals.

Response + Reason Predictor

1 · Target

Box the exact claim being answered.

2 · Responder

Use only that voice's claim and evidence.

3 · Polarity + reason

Agree, disagree, or qualify because...

4 · Boundary

Match group, condition, degree, time, and cause.
