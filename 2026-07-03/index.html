Joy SAT Tutoring - Class 7 Summary

Class date: July 3, 2026 | Focus: advanced punctuation review, parallelism, and comparison patterns

Class Content

  • We reviewed Joy's advanced punctuation homework mistakes from Lesson 6.
  • We started moving from simple word-level boundary recognition to a deeper sentence-structure level for punctuation decisions.
  • We spent extra time on dash questions. Joy learned that a comma can show a normal sequence, but a dash can show an interrupted or cut-off thought.
  • We also connected the review to Lesson 7 content: parallelism, lists, paired structures, and comparison logic.
  • For comparative and superlative questions, Joy noticed common fixed patterns such as as ... as, more ... than, and superlative forms.

Student Performance

  • Joy used a stronger sentence-structure approach today. She was more willing to find the sentence core and remove extra information before choosing an answer.
  • She explained punctuation choices more clearly than before, especially for dash vs. comma questions.
  • She also realized that comparative and superlative questions often follow fixed patterns, so this area needs more practice until the forms become automatic.
  • Across the last two weeks, Joy has built a clearer Standard English Conventions process: find the core sentence, identify extra information, check the grammar pattern, and then choose the answer.

Improvement Plan

  • Keep using the same grammar process for punctuation, parallelism, and comparison questions.
  • Add more short practice for comparative and superlative fixed patterns.
  • Because Joy's grammar process is now clearer, next week we can move to the tracker unit: Expression of Ideas - Transitions I.
  • For transitions, the next method will be: shorten the sentence, find anchor words, name the relationship, and choose the matching transition.
