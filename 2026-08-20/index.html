Class review

A concise recap of the grammar and poetry work completed on August 20.

August 20
Class contentWe worked through Opening Structures and the Real Main Clause. Joy reviewed four opening families and used the colour map to separate the opening structure from the real main-clause subject and finite verb. We then completed the six-question Literary Texts poetry handout, using Voice → Situation → Detail / Image → Shift → Meaning / Effect → Limit before the exact SAT question-type method. Tina also explained the remaining Form, Structure, and Sense mistakes.
Student performanceJoy stayed engaged, attempted answers, and accepted corrections. She could identify simple subjects and verbs and noticed several logical mismatches between an opening action and the noun that followed it. In denser sentences, she still needed support to ignore prepositional phrases and nearby modifiers, locate the true head subject, distinguish finite from nonfinite verbs, and explain her reasoning in a complete sentence.
Improvement planFor every opening, mark three things before choosing an answer: the opening structure, the person or thing it must describe, and the real main-clause subject + finite verb. For Form, Structure, and Sense, use the same subject-and-verb check instead of relying on what sounds natural. For poetry, complete the literary map first, then switch to the exact question-type routine and reject each close option at the field it breaks.
