August 7
Class contentWe compared a phrase with a complete sentence, identified a prepositional phrase, and mapped how independent, main, subordinate, and dependent clauses fit together. The examples also showed the comma pattern for two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction and the different patterns for clauses beginning with because or although.
Student performanceJoy worked through the class examples using the short labels NP, PP, IC, DC, MC, and SC. Handout 2 keeps those original notes together for review.
Improvement planBefore choosing punctuation, first decide whether each word group is a phrase, an independent clause, or a dependent clause. Then match the sentence to the comma pattern recorded in the class notes.
