Class review

The English review preserved from the class record.

July 6, 2026 · SAT Reading and Writing · Transitions I + Parallelism Review

Class Content

Reviewed previous parallelism and comparison mistakes, especially correlative structures such as “not only…but also,” “either/or,” and “as well as.”

Practiced finding the core subject, verb, and object before judging modifiers or comparison targets.

Introduced Transitions I: shorten the text into idea 1 and idea 2, find anchor words, name the relationship, then choose the transition.

Compared transitional connectors with simple connectors: transitional connectors label the relationship and are usually removable; simple connectors are part of the sentence structure.

Student Performance

Joy remembered several correlative conjunction patterns, identified basic parallelism in simpler contexts, and understood that a dependent clause cannot stand alone.

She also gave a clear explanation of transitional connectors, correctly noting that they can often be removed without destroying the core grammar of the sentence.

Main challenge: she still needs prompting to explain structural reasoning independently, especially when separating core sentence parts from modifiers or matching exact comparison targets.

Improvement Plan

Use one fixed routine for grammar questions: find the core sentence, remove extra modifiers, identify the tested structure, then explain why the answer works.

For parallelism and comparison, match the same kind of element on both sides: verb to verb, noun phrase to noun phrase, lifespan to lifespan, and population to population.

For transition questions, say the relationship aloud before choosing: same direction, example, contrast, cause/effect, time order, or restatement.

Homework

Review Class 7 parallelism/comparison mistakes, especially “A of B” vs. “B’s A” structures and matched comparison targets.

Complete the Transitions I 30-question practice and mark any question where the relationship is unclear.
