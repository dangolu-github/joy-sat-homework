Grammar Review · Web Recap

A compact review of the additional tense and comparison work archived around this class.

Tense

  • Use the event timeline: happening now, repeated, completed in the past, or completed before another past event.
  • Match continuous forms to actions in progress and perfect forms to relationships between times.

Comparison

  • Use the correct comparative form and keep both comparison targets logically equivalent.
  • Check fixed structures such as as … as, more … than, and one of the most …

Independent check

  • State the grammar job before filling the blank.
  • If two forms seem possible, identify the exact time clue or comparison frame that decides between them.
