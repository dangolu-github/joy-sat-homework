Joy Lesson 08: SAT-Style Transition Demonstrations

0706 Class 8

Annotation Key

Use the highlighted transition to check how idea 2 connects to idea 1.

ContrastExampleCause/effectSequenceRestatement

1. Contrast After A Previous Belief

For many years, marine biologists believed that the reef's recovery depended mainly on the number of adult corals that survived each storm; however, newer monitoring data show that the survival of tiny coral larvae may be a stronger predictor of long-term reef growth.

  • Idea before transition: old belief about adult corals.
  • Idea after transition: newer data point to coral larvae instead.
  • Relationship label: contrast between old belief and new evidence.

2. Example After A General Claim

Several cities are redesigning ordinary public spaces so they can serve ecological purposes; for example, a traffic median in Rotterdam was planted with grasses that absorb runoff before it reaches the sewer system.

  • Idea before transition: general claim about cities redesigning spaces.
  • Idea after transition: one specific city project.
  • Relationship label: example.

3. Cause And Effect

Because the archive's oldest recordings were stored on fragile wax cylinders, even a small change in temperature could damage them; therefore, the library moved the collection into a climate-controlled room before beginning digitization.

  • Cause: wax cylinders are fragile and temperature-sensitive.
  • Effect: the library moved the collection before digitizing it.
  • Relationship label: cause and effect.

4. Sequence In A Research Process

The geology team first used drones to photograph the landslide from above; then, after identifying the safest route on the images, the team entered the valley to collect rock samples by hand.

  • Anchor word before transition: first.
  • Idea after transition: the next step in the process.
  • Relationship label: sequence.

5. Restatement Of A Technical Idea

The material is hydrophobic, meaning that its surface repels water molecules; in other words, rain tends to bead on the coating rather than spread across it.

  • Idea before transition: technical definition.
  • Idea after transition: same idea in everyday visual terms.
  • Relationship label: restatement or clarification.
