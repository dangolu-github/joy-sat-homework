Class review

A concise recap of the mixed correction work and the new literary passage map.

August 18
Class contentWe reviewed Joy’s Rhetorical Synthesis mistakes and returned to Goal-Verb Lock → Note Filter → Sentence Fit Test. We then began checking the first ten Form, Structure, and Sense items. The correction was not finished; Tina used hints to help Joy identify the sentence structure and decide how it could be modified before testing the options. Finally, we worked through the Literary Texts handout and used Voice → Situation → Detail / Image → Shift → Meaning / Effect → Limit to organise fiction and poetry before applying the exact SAT question-type method.
Student performanceJoy responded to the questions and attempted to explain her reasoning. When she was unsure, her explanation was sometimes very brief or hesitant, so the next goal is to keep talking through the structure or relationship instead of stopping at “I’m not sure.” Her three submitted Transition practices were 31/33, 11/15, and 9/17; the drop across the sets shows that the method is not yet consistent under harder wording.
Improvement planFor Rhetorical Synthesis, state the exact goal, relationship, emphasis, and scope before reading the choices. For Form, Structure, and Sense, mark the subject, finite verb, and sentence structure before changing the form. For Literary Texts, complete the passage map first, then switch to the relevant question-type method. For Transitions, shorten the two ideas and name their relationship in plain English before choosing a connector.
HomeworkComplete Intensive Practice · Transitions Practice 4. It contains only the 14 missed questions from Practices 1–3, with the answer choices in a new order. Rebuild the relationship for every item and submit the full set once.
