Lesson 4 Homework: Advanced Punctuation 30Q Practice

Choose the answer that completes each sentence correctly.

Directions

  1. Mark the sentence core before choosing punctuation.
  2. Watch for over-punctuation: subject + verb, verb + object, and preposition + object should stay together.
  3. For comma or dash pairs, check where the inserted information opens and closes.
