Annotated SAT-Style Punctuation Demonstrations

Use these to practice seeing punctuation structure inside long sentences.

Color Key

Complete sentence means a subject and verb are present and the thought can stand alone.

Inserted information means the sentence still works if that section is removed.

No boundary means punctuation would split one grammar unit.

1. Colon introduces a specific explanation

The redesigned bus shelter solved a problem that the earlier model had created: passengers could now read the route map without standing in the rain.

Complete sentence
The redesigned bus shelter solved a problem that the earlier model had created
Boundary marker
Colon
Explanation
passengers could now read the route map without standing in the rain

2. Dash pair holds a removable insertion

The museum's textile samples—small strips removed from damaged costumes during earlier repairs—helped conservators identify the dyes used in the original production.

Main sentence
The museum's textile samples helped conservators identify the dyes
Inserted information
small strips removed from damaged costumes during earlier repairs
Boundary marker
Pair of dashes

3. Comma pair closes a nonessential which clause

The observatory's oldest telescope, which was built for a student astronomy club in 1912, still tracks lunar eclipses during public viewing nights.

Main sentence
The observatory's oldest telescope still tracks lunar eclipses
Nonessential clause
which was built for a student astronomy club in 1912
Boundary marker
Pair of commas

4. No punctuation inside a subject-verb unit

The collection of handwritten recipe cards from the community center reveals how immigrant families adapted familiar dishes to local ingredients.

Subject
The collection of handwritten recipe cards from the community center
Verb
reveals
No boundary
Do not separate subject from verb

5. Semicolon joins two complete clauses before however

The first version of the index listed every speaker alphabetically; however, the revised version grouped speakers by topic so readers could compare related interviews.

Independent clause 1
The first version of the index listed every speaker alphabetically
Transition
however
Independent clause 2
the revised version grouped speakers by topic

6. Such as already introduces examples

The workshop uses simple materials such as cardboard models, string grids, and transparent overlays to teach students how maps simplify space.

Main unit
The workshop uses simple materials
Example introducer
such as
Examples
cardboard models, string grids, and transparent overlays
